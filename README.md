# Bet Mitzvah Prayers

A practice page for the Shabbat morning prayers learned for a Bet Mitzvah. Each prayer is shown in Hebrew (with vowels), transliteration and English, with matching words color-coded across the columns. Page numbers refer to *Mishkan T'filah*, Shabbat edition.

Press play on a prayer or a line, or tap a word, to hear it read aloud with the device's built-in Hebrew voice. The word being spoken is highlighted in all three columns.

**Pages:**
- Daily prayers: https://mzeidenberg.github.io/bet-mitzvah-prayers/
- Torah portion, Miketz (Genesis 41:1–44:17), by aliyah: https://mzeidenberg.github.io/bet-mitzvah-prayers/miketz.html
- Haftarah for Miketz in 2026, Shabbat Hanukkah (1 Kings 7:40–50): https://mzeidenberg.github.io/bet-mitzvah-prayers/haftarah.html

The Torah and haftarah pages can show or hide the trope (cantillation) marks.

Notes:
- The audio is text-to-speech. It reads the words but does not chant the melodies or trope.
- The English is a literal, word-by-word translation written for these pages, not the one printed in Mishkan T'filah.
- Check the wording against the prayer book.
- Torah and haftarah Hebrew text: *Miqra according to the Masorah*, via Sefaria, licensed CC BY-SA.
